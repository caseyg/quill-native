Micropub clients

Quill and the other apps.

Any app that speaks Micropub can post to your site, so you’re never locked in to one. Here’s how Quill compares with the other clients for iPhone, Android, the web and the browser, and which of them are still around.

Checked on 18 November 2026 against the Indiekit client list, the IndieWeb wiki’s list of clients and each app’s own pages. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue.

Where Quill differs

What we couldn’t find anywhere else

Some of these lean on small Micropub extensions. Servers that don’t know them ignore them, and the post goes through as usual.

Different text for each network

Write one version for Bluesky and another for Mastodon, each counted the way that network counts, with @mentions of people you follow. Sent as mp-syndicate-text.

Threads

Split a long post into a thread for networks that support it, when your server advertises syndication-thread in its config.

Drafts on GitHub

Keep drafts as files in a GitHub repository you own, so they follow you between devices without a Quill server.

Voice notes

Record a thought and watch it turn into text as you speak, then post the text, the recording, or both.

And where others are ahead: OutPost, Micro.blog and iA Writer have Mac apps. Micro.blog and the late IndiePass include a reader for the people you follow. Quill on the web, Micropublish and Sparkles run in any browser with nothing to install. Indiekit comes with its own editor if it’s already your server. And Sparkles has watch, read, listen and play posts.

Every client we looked at

Grouped by where they run, with links to the sources for each claim above.

iPhone and iPad

OutPost

New

Jim Mitchell · iOS 18, macOS 15

The closest app to Quill. A Mac app launched in October 2026 and the iPhone and iPad app reached the App Store on 15 November 2026. Markdown with a preview, alt text prompts, editing any post on your site by URL, drafts, and syndication defaults per account.

SiteWiki

Micro.blog

Active

Manton Reece · iOS, Mac, Android

Built for Micro.blog first, but can sign in to your own Micropub or WordPress site and post there. WordPress custom post types aren’t supported. The old open-source iOS app was archived in 2023 when it was rewritten.

IndiekitWordPress helpOld source

Sunlit and Epilogue

Active

Micro.blog · iOS; Epilogue also on Android

Single-purpose apps from Micro.blog. Sunlit posts photo stories and Epilogue posts what you’re reading. Both can post to a Micropub site, with Micro.blog providing the timeline.

WikiClient list

iA Writer

Active

iA · iOS, Mac

A Markdown writing app that added Micropub publishing in version 5.5 (May 2020), beside WordPress, Ghost and Medium. Good for long articles, not for likes, replies or check-ins.

Drafts and Shortcuts

Active

Community actions · iOS, Mac

A Micropub action for Drafts and Shortcuts by Chuck Grimmett post notes, likes, replies and bookmarks. Handy glue if you set up your own token.

Gone

Eddie Hinkle · iOS

The first full native IndieWeb app for iPhone, with a Microsub reader and share sheet. Removed from the App Store in 2022, and the source is archived.

Android

IndiePass

Gone

Kristof De Jaeger, then Mark Sutherland · Android

Formerly Indigenous for Android, and the most complete Android client: a dozen post types, drafts, share target, a reader, and Mastodon and Pixelfed accounts. Removed from the app stores and archived on 21 January 2026.

Micro.blog for Android

Active

Micro.blog · Android

The official app, listed by Indiekit as able to post to any Micropub site. Since IndiePass left, it’s one of very few options on Android, which others have noticed too.

On the web

Quill

New

Aaron Parecki · any browser

The original, and the app this one is modelled on. A new version at quill.p3k.app is replacing quill.p3k.io, with Markdown articles, photo uploads, events, reviews, travel, food and weight posts, and bookmarklets for likes and reposts.

New siteSourceWiki

Micropublish

Active

Barry Frost · any browser

Strong on editing: create, update, delete and undelete, with templates for notes, articles, replies, RSVPs and more, and post status. No file uploads.

Source

Sparkles

Active

benji · any browser, installable

A cheerful, retro-styled client that installs as a web app and shows up in the share sheet. Notes, articles, photos and RSVPs, plus experimental watch, read, listen and play posts with movie and game lookup.

SourceWiki

editor.tanna.dev and Grumble

Active

Jamie Tanna; jjdelc · any browser

editor.tanna.dev shapes itself to the post types your server says it supports. Grumble works offline as a web app, with media and syndication, but needs CORS on your endpoints.

Client listGrumble source

Monocle

Active

Aaron Parecki · any browser

A reader rather than a writer. It reads from your Microsub server and posts likes and reposts back to your site with Micropub.

In the browser toolbar

Plume

New

rmdes · Chrome, Firefox

Released in 2026. A toolbar composer and right-click capture for notes, articles, bookmarks, replies, likes, reposts, quotes and photos. Several accounts, autosaved drafts and syndication targets.

SourceBuilding Plume

Omnibear

Active

Keith J. Grant, Anthony Ciccarello · Firefox, Edge

The long-standing extension for quick notes, replies, likes and reposts from the page you’re on. Being updated for current extension APIs since late 2024; Chrome support is to come.

SourceWiki

Built into your server

Indiekit

Active

Paul Robert Lloyd · Node.js server, any browser

Mainly a Micropub server that saves posts to GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, S3 or files for Eleventy, Hugo and Jekyll sites. Its posts plug-in adds a web editor: Markdown with image upload, alt text, drafts, location, scheduled dates, and edit, delete and restore. Syndicates to Bluesky, Mastodon and the Internet Archive. Version 1.0.0-beta.31, October 2026.

WikiPosts plug-inIts client list

Known

Active

Ben Werdmuller, Marcus Povey and others · PHP server, any browser

The IndieWeb publishing platform, now developed as Idno. Its editor writes posts, notes, photos, events and check-ins directly, with replies, likes and RSVPs by Webmention and syndication through plug-ins. It accepts Micropub from apps like Quill through its IndiePub plug-in, but its own editor doesn’t post by Micropub, so it isn’t in the table. It now federates over ActivityPub too. Version 1.6.4 came out in March 2026, and development is ongoing.
